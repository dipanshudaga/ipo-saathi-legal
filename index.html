Privacy Policy

IPO Saathi — last updated 29 September 2026

IPO Saathi helps you track Indian IPOs — issue dates, grey market premium, subscription figures — and check your own allotment status. This is a plain description of what the app actually does with your data.

What we collect

  • PAN numbers you add, to check allotment status. Stored only on your device, in its secure Keychain/Keystore storage — never uploaded to our servers. When you check allotment, your PAN is sent directly to the registrar handling that IPO (KFin, MUFG Intime, Bigshare, or Maashitla, depending on the issue) — the same lookup you'd do on that registrar's own website.
  • Notification preferences (which reminders you want), stored locally on your device.
  • Nothing else. No accounts exist yet, so we don't collect a name, email, or phone number through the app today.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, no ad tracking, no crash-reporting SDKs — the app doesn't phone home about how you use it.
  • We never sell or share your data. We don't have it to sell — PANs stay on your device.

IPO market data

Issue dates, price bands, subscription and grey-market figures come from NSE, BSE, SEBI, and IPO Guru's public feeds. This is public market data, not personal information about you.

Your control

You can remove a saved PAN at any time from the PANs tab. Uninstalling the app deletes everything, since nothing is stored anywhere but your device.

Changes

If we add accounts (phone-number sign-in) or any new data collection, this policy will be updated first, and that section will say plainly what's collected and why.
